# Card Art Direction — FINAL (Locked)

**Status:** `approved`  
**Style:** Stylized Semi-Realistic Fantasy Illustration  
**Authority:** `docs/final-art-direction.md` (Prompt 11 autonomous lock) · `docs/phase-11-owner-decisions.md`

## Rendering style

Collectible-card fantasy illustration between cartoon and photorealism. Painterly clarity, controlled detail, strong silhouette. Same technique language across all four suits.

## Anatomy rules

- Correct species anatomy is mandatory.
- No extra limbs, eyes, ears, horns, or jaws.
- Fantasy stylization may exaggerate presence, musculature, and expression — never erase species identity.
- Lion, Wolf, Fox, each bird, each reptile must read correctly at thumbnail size.

## Stylization strength

Medium: readable realism in form + fantasy lighting/atmosphere. Not chibi. Not stock photo.

## Lighting

Cinematic key + rim. Mysterious competitive mood. Legendary cards may use richer rim/atmosphere — not blown-out brightness.

## Eyes

Sharp, intentional, emotionally readable. Intelligent focus. No empty doll eyes. No horror gore.

## Fur / feathers / scales

- Fur: directional clumps, readable mass, not noisy photo grain.
- Feathers: layered plates, clean edge hierarchy.
- Scales: patterned plates with controlled specular; reptiles stay reptiles (not western dragons unless the card is a dragon).

## Magical effects

Allowed only as **controlled accents** (eye gleam, soft atmospheric bloom) on Legendary cards. No particle spam, no lens-flare stacks, no neon outlines.

## Saturation & contrast

Rich but disciplined. Suit atmosphere may tint backgrounds (crimson / sage / azure / violet). Avoid neon oversaturation.

## Atmospheric backgrounds

Gradients, fog, dust, embers, abstract habitat hints. Must not compete with the creature. No full scenic landscapes.

## Crop rules

- Default: head / head-and-neck portrait for card window (~3:4).
- Strong silhouette required.
- Concepts from metadata override defaults when present.

### Dragon of Moses escalation

| Rank | Card | Crop / scale |
|------|------|----------------|
| C | Dragon of Moses I | Full head + significant neck readable |
| B | Dragon of Moses II | Much larger head; frame feels barely enough |
| A | Dragon of Moses III | `extreme-scale-eye-only` — giant eye, scales, face fragment, shadow; **no full head** |

## Camera rules

Use manifest cameras: frontal, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, slight low angle, upward when appropriate. Avoid 52 identical frontal headshots.

## Negative / forbidden

Photography · childish cartoon · chibi · humanoid anatomy · corporate logo look · horror/gore · baked UI/text/numbers/borders/watermarks · excessive glow
